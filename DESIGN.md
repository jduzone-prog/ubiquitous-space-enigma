## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a sacred, contemplative, and editorial journey through Christian soteriology. Designed for reflection, deep scripture study, and theological progression, the aesthetic bridges monastic reverence with modern digital craftsmanship.

The visual direction draws from **Classical Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Illuminations**:
- **Warm Parchment Atmosphere**: High tactile warmth evoking centuries-old vellum, illuminated manuscripts, and timeless theological texts, eschewing sterile grays and stark whites.
- **Reverent Serenity**: Deliberate, breathing compositions that induce quiet contemplation rather than cognitive overload.
- **Sacred Accents**: Delicate golden amber highlights mimicking gilded illumination and celestial dawn, paired with earthy sage olive to represent spiritual growth and peace.
- **Typographic Gravity**: High-contrast, stately serifs for Latin and Korean theological titles paired with lucid, unobtrusive grotesque sans for doctrinal readings and biblical commentary.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in monastic materials: warm hand-pressed paper, iron-gall ink, gilded leaf, and olive leaf dye.

- **Primary (`#C59B27`)**: Antique Gilded Gold. Represents the uncreated divine light, grace, and eternal covenant. Used for active step nodes, illuminated dropped capitals, halo highlights, and progression markers.
- **Secondary (`#607254`)**: Liturgical Sage Olive. Symbolizes sanctification, renewal, peace, and spiritual growth. Used for reflective tags, secondary actions, scripture citations, and meditative milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#8C3A27`)**: Sacrificial Venetian Red. Applied sparingly for atonement callouts, urgent biblical warnings, and solemn historical cross-references.
- **Neutral (`#1E232A`)**: Deep Warm Obsidian. Replaces clinical black with deep, warm soot ink for body text, ensuring effortless sustained reading across morning and evening devotional hours.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`)**: Natural Alabaster Parchment. Provides an organic, soft reflective surface that reduces eye fatigue and establishes an atmosphere of sanctity.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` with `#F4F0E8` tints)**: Pristine vellum surfaces for elevated contemplation cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes rhythm between eternal scripture and doctrinal explanation:
- **Headlines & Display (Noto Serif)**: Brings solemnity, authority, and literary beauty. Used for the Latin/Korean phase names of *Ordo Salutis* (e.g., *Vocatio Efficax* / 효과적 소명), chapter titles, and prayer invocations.
- **Scripture Level (`scripture-quote`)**: Slightly amplified line height (30px on 17px type) creates an open, poetic cadence suitable for deep meditation.
- **Body & Commentary (Inter)**: Clean, balanced, neutral grotesque without idiosyncrasies, rendering dense theological treatises and commentaries effortlessly legible.
- **Theological Numerals & Badges (`label-theological-numeral`, `label-md`)**: Set with extended tracking in small caps or uppercase numerals (e.g., "STAGE I · ELECTION", "IX · GLORIFICATIO") to evoke classic chapter headings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces mobile-first contemplation, leaning on a fluid vertical axis that mirrors a pilgrimage route or ancient scroll unfolding.

- **Vertical Timeline Spine**: The central layout construct is an uninterrupted 2px vertical guide running down the screen, upon which the 9 steps of salvation are pinned.
- **Generous Section Margins (`margin` 1.25rem / 20px on mobile)**: Preserves breathing room against device glass, creating an internal "manuscript margin".
- **Spacious Rhythms**: Section transitions leverage `space-xl` (40px) to invite a deliberate mental pause before the user descends into the next theological phase.
- **Fluid Grid**: 4 columns on mobile, expanding to 8 columns on tablet with locked max reading container widths (640px) to prevent long line lengths in theological exposés.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic drop shadows, depth is achieved through **Illuminated Layering, Ambient Parchment Tones, and Gilded Inner Glows**:

1. **Base Tier (Background)**: Solid `#FAF8F5` parchment canvas.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Scripture & Commentary Cards)**: Warm pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a hair-thin perimeter boundary of `#EADECA` (35% opacity) and a soft ambient blur (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.02)`).
3. **Sacred Container Tier (Active Step Card)**: Subtle golden aura utilizing an inner glow (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.35), inset 0 1px 12px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`), imparting the feel of gold foil stamped onto smooth stock.
4. **Timeline Connectors**: Inactive links use soft, semi-transparent taupe lines (`#E2DDD5`); the completed/active path glows with a continuous 2px gradient moving from Sage Olive (`#607254`) into Gilded Gold (`#C59B27`).
5. **Modal / Contemplation Sheets**: Deep soft scrim (`rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.3)`) paired with floating surfaces bordered by gilded hairline edges.

## Shapes

A balanced curvature system (`roundedness: 2`) balances classic architectural arches with contemporary organic touchpoints:

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: 0.5rem (8px) corner radius, preserving the discipline of book covers and traditional printing plates.
- **Step Badges & Status Pills**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) to represent continuity, infinity, and sacred seals.
- **Ordo Salutis Node Icons**: Perfect circles with concentric rings (an outer 1px gold ring, a 4px breathing gap, and an inner solid gold center) representing eternal perfection.
- **Biblical Accent Dividers**: Subtle diamond or cross-point glyphs (`✦` or `✤`) at terminal endpoints of structural lines.

## Components

### 1. The Nine-Step Ordo Salutis Timeline Track
- **Timeline Node**: Circular icon (36px). Completed nodes feature a solid Sage Olive background with a crisp white checkmark or Latin numeral. Current active node is wrapped in a pulsating 2-ring golden halo (`#C59B27`). Upcoming nodes are outlined in delicate parchment gray (`#D8D2C7`).
- **Timeline Path**: 2px continuous line between nodes. Glows golden when progress is unlocked.

### 2. Sacred Theological Cards
- Structured container with 8px radius, white fill, 1px border (`#EBE6DD`), and padded with `space-lg`.
- Top header: Stage numeral in small caps (`label-theological-numeral`, e.g., "ORDO 03 · REGENERATIO"), accompanied by a gold or sage pill badge.
- Title: Korean & Latin combined title in `headline-md` (e.g., "중생 · Regeneration").
- Scripture Inset: Indented block with a left 2px gold accent border, styled with `scripture-quote` and an italicized citation label.

### 3. Buttons & Sacred Actions
- **Primary Button (Theological Advance / Complete Step)**: Deep obsidian fill (`#1E232A`) with gilded gold text (`#D4AF37`), or full gold fill (`#C59B27`) with dark ink text (`#1E232A`). Rounded 8px, uppercase tracked typography (`label-lg`), subtle inner bevel.
- **Secondary Button (Meditative Journaling / Prayer)**: Light sage wash (`#E9EDE6`) with deep sage text (`#607254`), 8px border radius, no drop shadow.
- **Ghost/Text Action**: Underlined serif action, hover/press state tinted with golden amber.

### 4. Input Fields (Contemplative Journaling)
- Minimalist open field: Off-white parchment fill (`#F5F2EB`), no harsh outer box—only a grounded 1px bottom border in `#D8D2C7`. Focus state shifts bottom line to 1.5px `#C59B27` with a soft golden glow.
- Cursor: Gold-tinted `#C59B27`.

### 5. Chips & Category Badges
- **Pillar Tags (Grace, Faith, Justification)**: Height 24px, pill-shaped. Background `#F2EFE9`, text `#607254` or `#C59B27`, 0.5px border matching text color at 25% opacity.

### 6. Illuminated Drop Caps
- Available for scripture commentary openings: 44px tall serif capital letter encased in a soft gold-tinted square container (`#FAF4E6`), wrapping the first 3 lines of body text.
